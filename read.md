## What I'm looking for

**This is not an SEO audit and it is not a tool.**
Search for “website audit” and you get software: a
crawler returns a score, a list of warnings, and no opinion. Those
are useful and they are free, and if that is what you want you
should use one. Most of them check conversion mechanics too —
button placement, headline formulas, page speed. Useful, narrow,
and not what this is.

The Read is a person watching your site the way a buyer does and
the way a machine does, at the same time, and saying which of the
two your business is losing to.

The Read looks at three things:

**Does the site carry your operating logic?** How
you actually work, who you actually serve, what actually makes
you different. Most sites carry a summary written years ago by
someone who no longer works there.

**Can machines read it?** Structure, not just content. Whether your pages let search and AI engines extract, understand, and quote the real story. Most don't, and it's invisible until you look.

**Does the story hold together?** One business,
one message, across every page. Or five eras of the company
layered on top of each other.
