Which one

  • Start with the Scan. It is free, it takes two minutes, and it tells you whether there is a problem worth paying to look at. Most people should not skip it.
  • The Read is for when you already know something is wrong and you want a person to say what, in what order, and what it is costing you. It is judgment, not a score — and it credits in full toward the Map if you go further.

Still deciding?

Neither one commits you to anything after it. If you want the work done for you, that is the second way in. If you would rather do it yourself, that is the third.
