Two questions, not eight options.

How much of me do you want, and what do you want to walk away with. Those are different questions, so they are different lists.

Work with me

By how much time.

Four sizes, smallest to largest. Nothing here produces a document; you are buying hours.

an hour The Session One call on the one thing that’s stuck. No build, no retainer. $250 a single day The Install In person, travel included. You end it able to do the work without me. $5,000 a few hours a month The Partner Someone owns your website. Requests shipped in three days, every month. From $950/mo a few days a month The Seat An operator in the seat, running operations rather than pages. From $6,000/mo
Programs

By what you leave with.

Four depths, in order. Each one is a thing you keep, and each credits in full toward the next.

a score The Scan What AI engines can read about you, scored. Free, and it commits you to nothing. Free · 2 minutes an opinion The Read A person watches your site the way a buyer and a machine both do, and ranks every fix. $450 · 5 days a plan The Map every build starts here How the business actually runs, on paper, with a fixed-price build proposal as the last page. $1,999 · 3 weeks the thing built The Site Your website rebuilt as the operating surface of the business. Priced by your Map · 6 to 12 weeks

The order is the point. A score tells you whether to look, an opinion tells you what is wrong, a plan tells you what to build, and the build is the last step rather than the first.
