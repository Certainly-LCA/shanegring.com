# What this site collects, and why.

What shanegring.com collects, why, and how to reach Shane about your data. Plain language: analytics, the contact form, the newsletter, and the free Scan.

Short version: shanegring.com is a personal site, not an ad business. It collects the minimum needed to
run analytics, answer the messages you send, and deliver the things you ask for. It does not sell your
data to anyone. This page explains what happens with each part. Last updated 24 August 2026.

## Analytics

When you visit on the live site (shanegring.com), Google Analytics 4 runs through Google Tag Manager to
count visits and see which pages get read. That involves cookies and a record of the pages you view, the
approximate region you visit from, and the kind of device and browser you use. It is used in aggregate to
understand what is working, not to identify you by name. Preview and local versions of the site do not send
analytics at all. You can block this with a browser setting, an ad blocker, or a Global Privacy Control
signal.

## The contact form

If you fill in the contact form, the name, email, company, and message you enter are sent through a form
handler (FormSubmit) and delivered to Shane's inbox at shane.gring@certainly.coop. That information is used
only to read and reply to what you sent. It is not added to a marketing list unless you separately ask to
subscribe.

## The newsletter

Subscribing to Seeking Certainty stores your email address with beehiiv, the service that sends the
newsletter. beehiiv handles the sending and keeps the usual open-and-click records so the emails work and
stay out of spam. Every issue has an unsubscribe link, and one click removes you for good.

## The free Scan

The Site Readiness Scan takes a website URL and, if you choose to send the result, an email address. The
URL is fetched and analysed to produce your report. If you ask for the report by email, the URL and your
email are recorded so Shane can send it and follow up. You are never required to give an email to see the
on-screen result.

## Who else is involved

The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages, which sees standard request logs. The services named above (Google,
FormSubmit, beehiiv, and, for the Scan, Google Workspace) each handle their own slice of data under their
own terms. No other party receives your information, and none of it is sold.

## Your choices

You can ask what data is held about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted. You can
unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time. To make any of these requests, or to ask a question about this
page, email [shane.gring@certainly.coop](mailto:shane.gring@certainly.coop) and it will be
handled directly.

This is a plain-language summary of real practice, not legal advice. If it changes,
the date at the top changes with it.
