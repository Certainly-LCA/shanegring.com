Partner vs. Install

The Partner means I do it. The Install means you can. Same underlying work, opposite answer to who's holding the keyboard in six months.

Read vs. Map

The Read is my opinion on your site, in five days. The Map is your business extracted onto paper, in three weeks, and it's where every build starts.

Partner vs. Seat

The Partner owns your website. The Seat owns your operations. One is a surface, one is a seat.

Two questions.

  • Is the problem that your website is neglected, or that it's wrong? Neglected is the Partner. Wrong is the Map.
  • Do you want the outcome, or the capability? Outcome is the Partner. Capability is the Install.

If you can't answer either, run the Scan. It's free and it takes two minutes, and the score usually answers both.
