## Whether this is for you

The Map is a poor fit more often than it is a good one, and both
of us find that out faster if the list is on the page.

### A good fit

- Founder-led or expert-led, with revenue and a team

- The way the work actually gets done lives in two or three heads

- You want the operating layer built, not a better-looking brochure

- A decision-maker will sit in all three sessions, not send a deputy

### Not the right fit

- What you want is a visual refresh of the site you have

- The real problem is demand, not operations

- Nobody inside the business thinks anything needs to change

- You want the memo, but you do not intend to act on it

## Questions

**Is this a website project?** No. It's an operating clarity project that usually reveals the website carries a fraction of the business. Some clients proceed to a build; others fix what they can with their existing setup, using the memo as the spec. Both are fine outcomes.

**We already have brand strategy docs. How is this
different?** Brand strategy describes how you want to
be seen. The Map documents how you actually run,
then derives the story from that. It's the difference between aspiration and extraction.

**Can my partner / CMO / ops lead join the
sessions?** Yes, and usually should. The founder is
required; up to two others are welcome. More than that and the
sessions turn into meetings, and meetings don't extract
anything.

**What happens to the AI and automation
findings?** Session two surfaces where tooling and automation would pay off, and the memo includes a ranked list with rough costs. If a build happens, that work gets scoped in. If not, the list stands on its own.

**What if I just want the build?** Every build starts with a Map. It's how I scope honestly and how you avoid rebuilding the wrong thing. No exceptions, including for people who offer to pay more to skip it.
