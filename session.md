## What people bring to a session

The questions I get most often aren't strategy questions. They're
the practical, load-bearing ones — the setup you only do once and
want to get right. A few that come up again and again:

**"How do I set up the entity?"** LLC, S-corp, or
cooperative — which one fits, how to form it, and the handful
of choices that actually matter later.

**"I can't see what's happening."** Wiring up
analytics — what to track, what to ignore, and how to get a
real read on the business instead of a wall of numbers.

**"I want to run this as a co-op."** Standing up a
cooperative — governance, membership, and the practical
mechanics, from someone doing it, not theorizing about it.

**"My tools are a mess."** Choosing and connecting
the operating stack — enough to run on, without over-buying
software you'll never grow into.

**"Everything runs through me."** The first
systems that get the business out of your head — where to start
so the team stops waiting on you for every call.

**"Where does AI actually help?"** The parts of
your operations a machine can now do, and the parts that still
need a person — a straight answer, not hype.

If it's an operational knot you'd want a second set of hands on,
it fits. When it's bigger than a call — when the answer is
"you need the systems built" — I'll tell you, and point you at
the right step of [the Path](https://shanegring.com/work-with-me).
