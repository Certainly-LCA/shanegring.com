# The questions behind founder-dependent websites, answered.

Plain-English guides on the questions expert-led businesses ask about their websites and operations: AI readability, content layers, service pages, schema, crawlers, and getting out of the middle of your own business.

These are the questions I answer on sales calls, in scan
reports, and inside engagements — **written out in full, holding
nothing back for the call**. Each one stands alone. Start with
**the one that sounds like the thing keeping you up**.
